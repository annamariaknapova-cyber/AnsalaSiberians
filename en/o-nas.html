About Us

Our Story

We are more than just a kennel — we are a family that lives for Huskies.

How it all began

Ansala Siberians was born from a pure love for the Siberian Husky — a breed that will steal your heart once and forever. We started as enthusiasts captivated by the beauty, intelligence, and free-spirited nature of this nordic breed.

Our story began in 2020 when we brought home our first dreamed-of puppy (Sansa), for which we waited 2 years, from the kennel Del Rey de la Montana Blanca. It was an extraordinary combination of several of the world's most recognised lines — Karnovanda, Hydrargium, and Kontoki.

Over time, we became part of a wide community of breeders and Husky lovers across Europe. Today we can be proud of several generations of show champions and, above all — happy dogs who are the heart of everything we do.

Our Breeding Philosophy

We believe quality breeding starts with solid foundations — healthy, typey parents with excellent temperaments. Every litter is an event for us, not a routine.

Puppies are raised in a home environment, in contact with children, people, and other animals, ensuring ideal socialisation from day one.

How our dogs live

Living Conditions

Our dogs live as full members of the family. They have access to the house, a large enclosure with plenty of space, and long walks or training in nature every day.

We place emphasis on mental stimulation — a Husky is an intelligent breed that needs not only physical activity but also mental engagement. We regularly participate in shows, canicross events, and sled racing.

We feed a combination of premium dry food and raw food (BARF). Vet checks are carried out regularly, all dogs are vaccinated, microchipped, and dewormed on schedule.

We believe happy and healthy parents equal happy and healthy puppies. That is why we invest in the quality of life of every dog in our care.
