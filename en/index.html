About Us

More than just a kennel

Ansala Siberians was born from a pure love for the Siberian Husky — a breed that will steal your heart once and forever. For us, our dogs are family.

We are a family-run breeding kennel with a passion for beautiful and courageous dogs. Our efforts focus on breeding healthy, happy, and high-quality dogs that are not only visually attractive but also have excellent temperaments.
