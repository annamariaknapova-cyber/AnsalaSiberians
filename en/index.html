About Us

More than just a kennel

Ansala Siberians was born from a pure love for the Siberian Husky — a breed that will steal your heart once and forever. For us, our dogs are not an investment or a business. They are family.

Our Story

Ansala Siberians has been operating in Slovakia with a deep passion for Siberian Huskies. We started as enthusiasts captivated by the beauty, intelligence, and free-spirited nature of this nordic breed. Over time, we became part of a wide community of breeders and husky lovers across Europe.

Our dogs participate in conformation shows, sled racing, and pulling sports — because a Husky needs movement, freedom, and meaningful activity. Every dog is tested for hereditary conditions (DM, HC, PRA) and has a verified pedigree.

Our Breeding Philosophy

We believe quality breeding starts with solid foundations — healthy, typey parents with excellent temperaments. Our puppies are raised in a home environment, in contact with children, people, and other animals, ensuring ideal socialisation from day one.

Every prospective owner goes through a short conversation — not to test you, but so we can together confirm that a Husky truly suits your lifestyle and expectations.

🏥

Health First

All breeding dogs undergo full genetic testing (DM, HC, PRA, MDR1) and are FCI registered.

🏡

Home Environment

Puppies grow up inside our home, not in kennels. Socialisation, human touch, and love from day one.

🏆

Show & Sport Achievements

Our dogs regularly place at international shows and pulling sport competitions.

❤️

Lifetime Support

Owners of Ansala puppies join our community. We're here for advice whenever you need us.

📋

Transparent Pedigree

Every puppy leaves with an FCI registration certificate, vaccination booklet, and full health records of both parents.

🌿

Ethical Breeding

A minimum number of litters per year. Females are never bred beyond recommended guidelines. Quality over quantity, always.

This is not a dog for apartment living without exercise — a Husky needs a minimum of 2 hours of active movement per day.
